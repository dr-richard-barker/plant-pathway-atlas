FAIR Principles & Standard Compliance

  • Findable: Registered in Zenodo (.zenodo.json) with permanent metadata and machine-readable CITATION.cff.
  • Accessible: Dual licensed (MIT for software, CC-BY-4.0 for data and maps); open standard formats (SVG, SBGN-ML PD, JSON).
  • Interoperable: Systems Biology Graphical Notation (SBGN-ML libsbgn 0.3) compliant with MapMan/PlantCyc annotations; validated against JSON Schema.
  • Reusable: Complete MANIFEST.tsv and cryptographic CHECKSUMS.sha256; automated ABAI Quality Control (QC) screening (.abai/attest.json).
